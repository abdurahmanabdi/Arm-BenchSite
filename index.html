What went wrong in task 2

Two distinct failures, documented in the project's field report rather than smoothed over.

The fling. Cube A was transported in a single large IK move. The grip was firm but not maximal, and the resulting swing was fast enough that the cube came free mid-flight and landed 1.2 meters away — outside the arm's reach for good. Fixed by breaking transport into a lift, capped-step translate, and descend sequence, instead of trusting a single long jump.

Death by a thousand nudges. Cube A was placed cleanly. While aligning cube B above it for the stack, the gripper's fingertips — which extend about 3cm past the point the code was tracking — clipped cube A on each descent. Four small, individually-plausible nudges in the same direction accumulated until the cube was knocked off the table. No single correction was the mistake; the mistake was treating each nudge as independent instead of noticing the trend.

Replaying the actual telemetry from this run: the old detector only fired once cube A was already flung off the table. A detector that also tracks drift direction, not just magnitude, flags the same trend one step earlier — while the cube was still recoverable.
